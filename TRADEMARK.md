# Orientis Digital — Trademark & Brand Policy

**Effective Date:** August 2026  
**Applies to:** Vexta, Vexta Messenger, Vexta Bridge, Vexta Mobile, and associated Orientis Digital projects.

---

## 1. Overview & Relationship to Open-Source Licenses

The source code of Vexta projects is released under the permissive [MIT License](./LICENSE). The MIT License grants broad rights to copy, modify, and distribute the software code.

However, **open-source software licenses do NOT grant trademark rights**. 

This Trademark Policy outlines the rules for using the names, logos, domains, and branding elements associated with **Orientis Digital** and **Vexta**. The goal of this policy is to prevent user confusion, protect community trust in official releases, and ensure that anyone using official Vexta software receives authentic, untampered cryptographic builds.

## 3. Permitted Uses (No Prior Permission Required)

You may use the Vexta word marks without prior written permission in the following contexts, provided there is **no risk of confusion or implied endorsement**:

### ✅ A. Truthful Technical References (Nominative Fair Use)
* You may state that your project, plugin, bot, or client is compatible with or based on Vexta.
  * **Allowed**: *"A community bridge client compatible with Vexta Relay protocol."*
  * **Allowed**: *"Built on the open-source Vexta codebase."*
  * **Not Allowed**: *"Official Vexta Pro Client"* or *"Vexta Mobile Enterprise"*.

### ✅ B. Linking & Community Promotion
* You may use the Vexta name and official unmodified logo to link directly to official Vexta repositories, documentation, or download portals (`https://downloads.nexusec.space`).

### ✅ C. Research, Auditing & News
* You may reference Vexta in articles, academic publications, security audit reports, and reviews.

## 4. Restrictions on Modified Builds & Forks

To preserve the security guarantees of the Vexta cryptographic ecosystem:

### ❌ Rebranding Required for Modified Builds
If you modify the source code (including but not limited to changing cryptographic primitives, server endpoints, or user interface components) and distribute binary releases to the public:
1. **You must remove the official Vexta and Orientis logos and application icons.**
2. **You must choose a distinct, non-confusing name for your project** (e.g. *"SecurComm, powered by Vexta architecture"*).
3. You must not present the modified build as official Vexta software.

### ❌ No Misleading Association or Endorsement
* You may not use Orientis Digital or Vexta trademarks in any manner that falsely implies sponsorship, certification, or official endorsement by Orientis Digital.

### ❌ Prohibited Commercial Exploitation of Brand
* You may not sell merchandise, commercial services, or paid subscriptions branded as "Vexta" or "Orientis" without an explicit commercial trademark license.

## 5. Domain & Official Relay Infrastructure Guidelines

* **Domain Registration**: You may not register domain names containing `vexta` or `orientis` as part of a commercial service or misleading community portal (e.g., `vexta-download.com`, `official-vexta.org`).
* **Official Relay Usage**: Third-party applications, bots, and forks are not entitled to consume bandwidth on official production relays (`vexta-api.nexusec.space`, `downloads.nexusec.space`) without authorization. Independent relay operators must configure and host their own bridges.
